ABOUT

I'm a Multimedia Design student with a strong interest in visual communication, graphic design and digital experiences. I enjoy taking an idea from the first visual direction to the final result — whether that means developing a brand identity, designing a campaign, creating an interface or building the solution itself. I'm drawn to bold visual concepts, thoughtful details and design that has a clear purpose. I like experimenting, learning by doing and moving between the creative and technical sides of a project.
